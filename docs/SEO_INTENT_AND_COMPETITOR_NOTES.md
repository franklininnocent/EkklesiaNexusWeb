# SEO intent and competitor notes (internal, Oct 2026)

**Not traffic forecasts.** Classification from SERP review and product inventory. Homepage testimonials, feature cards, and demonstration pricing remain frozen; this doc guides crawlable landing pages only.

## Branded

- Query `EkklesiaNexus church management` did not surface `ekklesianexus.com` in sampled results.
- Confusable names: Ekklesia Church Solution, Ekklesia Nigeria, FaithNexus, etc.
- Mitigation: consistent Organization JSON-LD, About/Contact, official domain in titles—never competitor trademarks as keywords.

## Category (generic ChMS)

- US/global SERP leaders sell attendance, apps, volunteers, messaging—capabilities EkklesiaNexus does not ship.
- Homepage targets honest Catholic-parish positioning; depth lives on `/features/*` and `/solutions/catholic-parishes`.

## Catholic / sacramental

- Compete on household registers, sacramental books, certificates, Mass intentions, BCC wards, parish stewardship office workflows.

## India parish software

- Do not copy UPI/mobile-app/attendance claims. Differentiate on family-first office workflows, registers, intentions, ministries/BCC, stewardship collections/receipts, RBAC/tenant isolation (English-first).

## Pricing SERP

- Competitors use `/pricing` URLs. Public homepage `#pricing` stays demonstration data until separately approved; catalog API powers `/preview/plans` only.
